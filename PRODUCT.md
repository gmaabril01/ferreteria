# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/vanilla JS (classic `defer` scripts, IIFE, no build step), Three.js + GSAP ScrollTrigger vendored
locally, native scroll. Deploy target: Hostinger (drag-and-drop of `sitio/`). Chosen by the user (approach A).

## Users

Two audiences in equal measure, both on the island of Lanzarote:
- Homeowners doing repairs or renovations (reformas y arreglos en casa) who need advice and to find everything in one place.
- Trade professionals (builders, plumbers, pool installers) who need stock, brands and a reliable supplier.
Their job on the site: decide to go to (or call) the right store, and trust that it will have what they need.

## Product Purpose

One-page website for Suministros José Luis Cabrera S.L., a family hardware, paint and glass supplier.
Success = visitors find the right location/opening hours and call or get directions; the business ranks for local searches
(ferretería, pintura, vidrio, piscinas, fontanería, puertas de garaje en Tías / Playa Blanca / Lanzarote).

## Positioning

Canarian family business born in the 1990s, specialists in garage doors and automation, pools, plumbing, PVC pipe,
hardware, casting and construction. Two sales points plus a door/automation showroom on the island.
Customers say that if something is missing, they get it for you.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Only sales and in-store advice may be claimed. Installation, made-to-measure glass cutting and paint tinting are
  NOT confirmed — never mention them.
- Client-provided texts (Sobre nosotros, three Google reviews) must be reproduced literally.
- Domain undecided (placeholder suministrosjoseluiscabrera.es). Legal pages (aviso legal, privacidad) out of scope.

## Product Principles

1. Get people to the right door: location, hours and phone are never more than one tap away.
2. Show real stock and real stores; the photos are the proof.
3. Speak to both the homeowner and the professional: plain words, concrete product names.
4. Never overclaim: only what the client confirmed.
